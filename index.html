Amber Jiayu Su

Amber Jiayu Su graduated from Cornell University with Bachelor of Architecture degree.

At Cornell, Amber conducts research at the Environmental Systems Lab, focusing on urban building energy modeling and retrofitting strategies. Her work explores holistic design approaches that integrate building performance, occupant comfort, and urban context. The goal of her research is to support cities in addressing the challenges posed by climate change and shifting environmental conditions.

Amber also worked as an environmental design intern at KPF, focusing on thermal comfort, and building energy modeling.
